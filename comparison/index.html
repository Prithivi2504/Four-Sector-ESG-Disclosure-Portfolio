ANALYST NOTE

Use sector-specific denominators where possible.

Manufacturers can report per tonne of steel or per equivalent vehicle; IT firms may report per FTE; banks may report per ₹ crore of revenue or per FTE. A single revenue-intensity comparison can conceal business-model differences.
