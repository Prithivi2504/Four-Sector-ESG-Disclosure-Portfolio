REPORTING BASIS & BOUNDARY

What is included?

The selected values describe the Bank's operational boundary (branches, offices, vehicles, DG sets and other operational sources). They are not the Bank's financed emissions portfolio. HDFC Bank describes its Scope 3 categories separately, including selected operational/value-chain categories.

Scope 2 methodology

Market-based Scope 2 is used for the Scope 1+2 intensity (0.89 tCO2e/₹ crore). The report also discloses location-based Scope 2 at approximately 0.30 million tCO2e; the two methods must not be mixed in a single total.

Assurance qualification

The annual report contains BRSR Core assurance/assessment disclosures. Assurance is metric- and boundary-specific; consult the bank's assurance statement before claiming that every environmental figure is assured.

RESOURCE & WASTE DETAIL

Data quality notes

Water: Withdrawal Not separately disclosed in selected source KL. Consumption 519,272.97 KL. The Bank describes this as an estimated total water consumption, with metering installed at select larger facilities. Do not treat it as a fully metered figure for every premise.

Waste recovery: 312.82 tonnes. Disposal: 3,764.24 tonnes.

The company table calls 312.82 tonnes waste recycled and 3,764.24 tonnes diverted to disposal. The 7.67% is a simple derived recycled/generated ratio, not a company-labelled total recovery rate.

• Bank operational emissions are not a substitute for financed-emissions reporting.
• Scope 2 market-based and location-based values differ materially; this comparison uses market-based for the reported intensity and displays location-based separately.
• Water consumption is reported as an estimate; water meters are installed at select larger facilities.
• Revenue from operations and the bank's business model differ from industrial manufacturers, so intensity comparisons are descriptive only.
