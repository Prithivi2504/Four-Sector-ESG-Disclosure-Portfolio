REPORTING BASIS & BOUNDARY

What is included?

The FY26 Integrated Report describes a broader ESG reporting boundary covering Tata Steel and key subsidiaries/associates/JVs, while the BRSR schedules separately present standalone and consolidated values. This dashboard selects the standalone BRSR columns for company-level consistency. Do not mix the group narrative boundary with standalone KPI rows.

Scope 2 methodology

Scope 2 in the selected BRSR KPI table is presented on a location-based basis. Emissions are disclosed in rounded whole million tonnes; totals and derived intensities are therefore approximate when recalculated.

Assurance qualification

The report states reasonable and limited assurance on agreed Integrated Report/BRSR ESG parameters on a standalone basis unless otherwise stated. It specifically excludes the Scope 1+2 intensity per rupee of turnover from the stated assurance subset. Check the assurance statement before using any indicator for a formal assurance claim.

RESOURCE & WASTE DETAIL

Data quality notes

Water: Withdrawal 110,623,000.00 KL. Consumption 99,055,000.00 KL. Water values normalized to KL where the company reported million litres.

Waste recovery: 18,842,244.00 tonnes. Disposal: 21,909.00 tonnes.

The report's total recovery (18,842,244 t) exceeds current-year waste generated (18,782,249 t). The company reports a waste recovery intensity of 1.003. Do not calculate this as a simple same-year recycling percentage or force recovery plus disposal to equal current-period generation.

• Primary and secondary energy totals are both reported; this project consistently uses the standalone secondary-energy column (FY26: 623.81 PJ).
• Scope 1 and Scope 2 are rounded to whole MtCO2e; avoid false precision in recalculated totals.
• Waste recovery can exceed same-year waste generation; this may reflect stock/legacy material accounting, but the report does not specify the cause. Do not present recovered/generated as a conventional recycling rate.
