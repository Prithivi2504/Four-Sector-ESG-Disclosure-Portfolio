REPORTING BASIS & BOUNDARY

What is included?

The selected BRSR figures relate to Mahindra & Mahindra Limited. The company operates automotive and farm-equipment businesses; it is used here as the automotive-sector representative, not as a pure automotive-only business. Keep company-level totals distinct from business-unit-specific intensity disclosures.

Scope 2 methodology

The FY26 BRSR Core verified-data extract identifies the selected Scope 2 KPI as market-based. The comparison preserves this reported basis and does not relabel it as location-based.

Assurance qualification

DNV Business Assurance India Private Limited is identified as the external assurance/assessment provider for selected reported indicators and BRSR Core data. This does not mean every disclosure in the annual report is assured.
