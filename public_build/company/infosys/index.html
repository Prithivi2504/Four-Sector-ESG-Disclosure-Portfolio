REPORTING BASIS & BOUNDARY

What is included?

The BRSR identifies the reporting year April 2025–March 2026. Environmental disclosures cover global offices/operations with indicator-specific qualifications. Scope 1 includes owned offices in India, US and China and leased offices in India; leased overseas space is excluded as de minimis for diesel/natural gas. Scope 2 includes India and overseas locations.

Scope 2 methodology

Market-based Scope 2 is 34,351 tCO2e; corresponding location-based Scope 2 is 145,604 tCO2e. Scope 1 + market-based Scope 2 is the reported intensity basis.

Assurance qualification

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP is named as the independent assessment/assurance provider in the BRSR data tables. Do not infer that every qualitative statement or all KPIs receive identical assurance; review the assurance statement and scope.
