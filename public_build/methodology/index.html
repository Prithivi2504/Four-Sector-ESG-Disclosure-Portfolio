COMPARABILITY LOG

Known limitations

01
Cross-sector comparison

The four entities use different business models, operational scales and reporting boundaries. Absolute emissions, energy, water and waste are displayed as reported but must not be used as an overall sustainability ranking.

02
GHG Scope 2 method

Tata Steel's selected BRSR Scope 2 is location-based. Mahindra's selected BRSR Core figure is market-based. HDFC Bank and Infosys disclose market-based and location-based Scope 2; the market-based figure is used for their reported Scope 1+2 intensity and the alternative is shown separately.

03
Emissions units

Tata Steel reports million tonnes CO2e rounded to whole numbers; the system converts Mt to metric tonnes for display but preserves the rounding caveat. The other three companies report metric tonnes CO2e in the selected disclosures.

04
Revenue intensity

Revenue-based intensity is shown as company-reported or unit-converted, but denominator definitions and sector economics differ. Do not compare the intensity values as a universal efficiency league table.

05
Water units

Tata Steel reports in million litres (ML); other companies report in kilolitres (KL). The website preserves source units and separately converts Tata ML to KL using 1 ML = 1,000 KL.

06
Renewable energy share

Tata Steel and HDFC Bank publish renewable share percentages. Mahindra and Infosys percentages are recalculated from the source renewable / total GJ values and are labelled as calculated.

07
Waste reconciliation

Tata's total recovery exceeds same-year generated waste. Infosys' selected generated, recovered and disposed totals leave a 522.43 t difference. Neither is forced into a misleading recycling rate or allocated to an invented category.

08
Assurance

Assurance is indicator-, boundary- and level-specific. The presence of an assurance provider in a report does not mean every disclosure in the report is assured.

09
Scope limits

This is a selected-data portfolio benchmark, not a complete BRSR reproduction or regulatory compliance opinion. Selected social and governance indicators are now shown where source data and denominators were located. Some indicators are not included in the comparison where the selected company source set did not provide a sufficiently comparable value. Missing values are not treated as zero.

10
Social metrics and workforce definitions

Workforce definitions differ: Tata Steel employee count is company-reported; Mahindra reports employees and workers as distinct populations; HDFC Bank workforce gender share is reported without a comparable headcount in this selected table; Infosys reports its global consolidated workforce as employees. Do not rank these figures without reconciling definitions and boundaries.

11
Board diversity

Female board shares are shown either as company-reported or derived from female directors / total directors at the reporting date. Board size and reporting dates must be checked against each company report before making a governance comparison.

12
Occupational safety and POSH

LTIFR is shown only with its stated denominator (employees/workers and frequency basis). POSH complaint counts are not employee-normalized here. A blank means not included in this selected data pack, not zero complaints or zero incidents.

13
Scope 2 method-specific comparison

The comparison now shows market-based and location-based Scope 2 in separate columns. Tata Steel’s selected standalone value is location-based; Mahindra & Mahindra’s selected BRSR Core figure is market-based and a separate location-based figure was not identified; HDFC Bank and Infosys disclose both methods. Location-based totals are calculated as Scope 1 plus location-based Scope 2 only where a company value is disclosed. HDFC Bank’s 0.30 million tCO₂e location-based figure is approximate.

14
Tata Steel intensity precision

Tata Steel’s reported intensity is 0.0005 million tCO₂e per ₹ crore, rounded in the source, while Scope 1 and Scope 2 are rounded to whole million tonnes. The converted value is therefore displayed as ≈500 tCO₂e per ₹ crore, not 500.00.
